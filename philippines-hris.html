From Spreadsheets and Scattered Tools to an All-in-one HR System

If you're still using spreadsheets, free tools and email chains to run HR; it's costing you time, accuracy and control. Too many tools, too much manual work means too many mistakes. PeoplesHR replaces all of that with one system. One login. Zero confusion.

What you get with PeoplesHR

An all-in-one HR system

Leave, attendance, performance, documents; everything in one cloud platform, accessible anytime, anywhere.

Self-Service that actually works

Employees can apply for leave, check balances, access documents or update profiles without pinging HR 10 times.

Real-time Dashboards

From headcount to absenteeism to turnover predictions, you'll have instant insights that don't live in a spreadsheet.

Automated Workflows

From onboarding to asset handover, approvals, reminders and exits; we automate the boring stuff, so you don't have to babysit processes.

Secure, Searchable, Audit-Proof

All employee records stored securely, with access control, logs and backups. No more guessing what's updated and what's not.

Strongest Payroll Software that plays by the Philippine rules

Let's face it, Philippine payroll isn't for the faint-hearted. Between BIR, SSS, PhilHealth, Pag-IBIG and all those shifting tax rules, it's easy to feel like you need a law degree just to run payroll. We've built a system, so you don't have to.

Stay 100% aligned with DOLE, BIR, SSS, PhilHealth and Pag-IBIG. Tax tables, ₱90K exemptions, contribution rules: all built in and updated for you.
Auto-calculate 13th month pay even for resignations, new joiners or maternity cases. Generate BIR 2316, 1601-C, Pag-IBIG files and more instantly.
From split shifts to half-day no-pay OT, payroll syncs directly with attendance, rosters and leave logs. Zero manual adjustments.
Payslips show YTD earnings, OT, night differentials and deductions. Paysheets break it all down by hours, rates and tax assumptions.
Hourly, salaried, contract, shift-based or commission: we calculate it all down to the last peso, with no surprises.
Employees can view payslips, request updates and check balances anytime, anywhere, from their phones.
