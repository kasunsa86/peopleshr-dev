How take-home salary is calculated in Sri Lanka

Net salary is gross earnings minus the employee's 8% EPF contribution, APIT (often still called PAYE) and any other deductions. On top of the gross salary, the employer pays 12% EPF and 3% ETF, so the total cost of an employee is higher than their gross pay.

Sri Lanka salary calculator FAQs

What is the tax-free salary in Sri Lanka for 2026/27?

Employees get personal relief of Rs. 1,800,000 a year, which is Rs. 150,000 a month. A monthly taxable salary up to Rs. 150,000 has no APIT deducted.

How is APIT calculated for 2026/27?

Income above the Rs. 1,800,000 personal relief is taxed at 6% on the first Rs. 1,000,000, then 18%, 24% and 30% on each next Rs. 500,000, and 36% on the rest. These rates took effect on 1 April 2025 and no changes have been announced for 2026/27. Monthly APIT under Table 01 spreads the annual bands evenly across 12 months. See the Inland Revenue Department APIT tables.

Is APIT the same as PAYE?

Yes, for salaries. APIT (Advance Personal Income Tax) replaced PAYE under the Inland Revenue Act No. 24 of 2017. Many people still call it PAYE. It's the income tax employers deduct from monthly salary.

Is EPF deducted before APIT is worked out?

No. The employee's 8% EPF contribution doesn't reduce taxable employment income, so APIT is calculated on the full taxable salary. Contribution rules are published by the Employees’ Provident Fund.

Which earnings count for EPF and ETF?

EPF and ETF are based on "total earnings": basic salary plus allowances such as cost-of-living, special and fixed allowances. Overtime and certain other payments are excluded. Use the EPF/ETF tick on each allowance to match how your organisation treats it. For guidance, see the Department of Labour, the EPF and the Employees’ Trust Fund Board.

How does the net to gross salary calculator work?

Enter the take-home salary you want to pay. The calculator keeps your allowances and deductions as entered and finds the basic salary that produces that net amount after EPF, APIT and deductions.

Are bonuses included?

No. Bonuses, arrears and other lump-sum payments are taxed under separate APIT tables. This calculator covers regular monthly pay from primary employment only.

Sri Lanka payroll reference

Rates & Rules

APIT, Y/A 2026/27

  • Same rates as Y/A 2025/26 (effective 1 April 2025); no changes announced for 2026/27.
  • Personal relief: Rs. 1,800,000 a year (Rs. 150,000 a month).
  • 6% on the first Rs. 1,000,000 of taxable income after relief.
  • 18%, 24% and 30% on each of the next three Rs. 500,000 bands.
  • 36% on the balance.
  • Based on Table 01: regular monthly profits from primary employment, resident employees.

Source: IRD APIT Table 01 (2025/26) · All APIT tables

Monthly APIT bands

  • Up to Rs. 150,000: 0%
  • Rs. 150,000 – 233,333: 6%
  • Rs. 233,333 – 275,000: 18%
  • Rs. 275,000 – 316,667: 24%
  • Rs. 316,667 – 358,333: 30%
  • Above Rs. 358,333: 36%

Employees' Provident Fund (EPF)

  • Employee: 8% of total earnings, deducted from salary.
  • Employer: minimum 12% of total earnings (some employers pay 15%).
  • The employee contribution doesn't reduce income for APIT.

Source: Employees’ Provident Fund · Department of Labour

Employees' Trust Fund (ETF)

  • Employer only: 3% of total earnings.
  • Nothing is deducted from the employee's salary.

Source: Employees’ Trust Fund Board
