How take-home pay is calculated in Indonesia

Net pay is gross pay minus the employee's BPJS contributions (JHT, JP and BPJS Kesehatan), PPh 21 and any other deductions. From January to November, PPh 21 is withheld at a single TER rate applied to gross income, which includes the employer's JKK, JKM and BPJS Kesehatan premiums. In December the full-year tax is worked out under Article 17 and the balance is withheld. On top of gross pay, the employer pays its own BPJS shares.

Frequently asked questions

How is PPh 21 calculated each month?

Since January 2024, employers withhold PPh 21 for January to November using the average effective rate (Tarif Efektif Rata-rata, TER) set by PP 58/2023 and PMK 168/2023. The TER rate is applied to the monthly gross income, which includes salary, taxable allowances and the JKK, JKM and BPJS Kesehatan premiums paid by the employer. There are three TER categories, A, B and C, based on PTKP status. See the Directorate General of Taxes.

Why is December withholding different?

In December (or the employee's final month), the employer recalculates the full year's tax with the Article 17 progressive rates: 5%, 15%, 25%, 30% and 35%. The tax already withheld under TER is subtracted, so December can be noticeably higher or lower than other months. The overview shows the annual tax and the estimated December amount.

How are BPJS contributions worked out?

BPJS Ketenagakerjaan covers JHT (old-age savings, 3.7% employer and 2% employee), JP (pension, 2% employer and 1% employee, on wages up to a cap that is revised each March), JKK (work accident, 0.24% to 1.74% employer by risk level) and JKM (death, 0.3% employer). BPJS Kesehatan is 5% of wages up to Rp 12,000,000, with 4% from the employer and 1% from the employee. See BPJS Ketenagakerjaan and BPJS Kesehatan.

What is PTKP?

PTKP (Penghasilan Tidak Kena Pajak) is the annual non-taxable income allowance. It is Rp 54,000,000 for a single taxpayer, plus Rp 4,500,000 if married and Rp 4,500,000 for each dependant, up to three. PTKP status also sets the TER category used for monthly withholding.

How does Net to Gross work?

Enter the take-home pay you want to pay. The calculator keeps your allowances and deductions as entered and finds the basic salary that produces that net amount after BPJS contributions, PPh 21 under TER and deductions.

Is THR included?

No. The religious holiday allowance (Tunjangan Hari Raya, THR), bonuses and other irregular payments are added to gross income in the month they are paid, which can push that month into a higher TER rate. This calculator covers regular monthly pay only.

Indonesia payroll reference

Rates & Rules

PPh 21 monthly withholding (TER), January 2024 onwards

  • Category A: TK/0, TK/1 and K/0. 0% up to Rp 5,400,000 gross a month.
  • Category B: TK/2, TK/3, K/1 and K/2. 0% up to Rp 6,200,000.
  • Category C: K/3. 0% up to Rp 6,600,000.
  • Rates rise through each table to 34% at the top. The rate is applied to the whole monthly gross income, including employer-paid JKK, JKM and BPJS Kesehatan premiums.
  • Used for January to November. December uses the annual Article 17 calculation.

Source: Directorate General of Taxes (PP 58/2023, PMK 168/2023)

Annual PPh 21, Article 17 (UU HPP)

  • Up to Rp 60,000,000: 5%
  • Rp 60,000,000 – 250,000,000: 15%
  • Rp 250,000,000 – 500,000,000: 25%
  • Rp 500,000,000 – 5,000,000,000: 30%
  • Above Rp 5,000,000,000: 35%
  • Deductions: occupational cost (biaya jabatan) of 5% up to Rp 6,000,000 a year, employee JHT and JP contributions, and PTKP.
  • PTKP: Rp 54,000,000, plus Rp 4,500,000 if married and Rp 4,500,000 per dependant (max 3).

Source: Directorate General of Taxes (UU 7/2021 HPP)

BPJS Ketenagakerjaan

  • JHT: 3.7% employer, 2% employee.
  • JP: 2% employer, 1% employee, on wages up to Rp 11,086,300 (cap from 1 March 2026, revised each March).
  • JKK: 0.24%, 0.54%, 0.89%, 1.27% or 1.74% employer, by workplace risk level.
  • JKM: 0.3% employer.

Source: BPJS Ketenagakerjaan

BPJS Kesehatan

  • 5% of monthly wages: 4% employer, 1% employee.
  • Wage cap Rp 12,000,000, so the employee share is at most Rp 120,000.

Source: BPJS Kesehatan
