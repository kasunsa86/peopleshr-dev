Deadlines that fall on a weekend move to the next working day (BIR RMC No. 65-2016). eFPS filers follow their group's staggered dates. The calendar file adds a reminder the day before each date.

03 · What changed since last year-end

Four updates that affect this year-end

  1. De minimis ceilings went up

    RR 29-2025 raised them from January 6, 2026: rice to ₱2,500 a month, Christmas gifts to ₱6,000 a year and more. See the rates & rules.

  2. Two big BIR deadlines land on a Sunday

    The 1604-C and alphalist move to Monday, February 1, 2027. The BIR copy of Form 2316 moves to Monday, March 1, 2027.

  3. Unsigned 2316s won't pass

    The BIR's copy must carry the employee's signature (RMC 29-2024), sent with the notarized Annex C and Annex F.

  4. Minimum wages went up in NCR and CALABARZON

    NCR is now ₱755 from September 26, 2026 (Wage Order NCR-28), while NCR-27 is on hold in court. In CALABARZON, Wage Order IVA-23 adds ₱25 to ₱45 a day, up to ₱645, from 15 days after publication.

Still paying the ₱500 Annual Registration Fee? You can drop it. It was abolished in January 2024 under the Ease of Paying Taxes Act (RA 11976).
