WEBINAR SUMMARY · HR & PEOPLE

From transactional to strategic: how AI can help HR in Africa step up

FORMATOn-demand webinar
LENGTH53 min
CONDUCTED ONWed, 28 August 2024
LANGUAGEEnglish

Co-hosted with Global Frontiers and ADP Advisory Services, Tanzania. Luxsho Logan of PeoplesHR shows where AI already helps with recruitment, onboarding and learning, and how to adopt it safely. Tarvinder Puri then explains why AI can help HR in Africa move from carrying out instructions to shaping strategy.

WATCH THE RECORDING AND LEARN HOW TO

1. Apply AI to recruitment, onboarding and learning

2. Adopt AI tools one area at a time

3. Answer the privacy and regulation questions first

Key takeaways

Watch from 06:10

Early adopters are already ahead. Gartner found 76% of HR leaders expect to lag competitors without AI in 12 to 24 months, and early reports from McKinsey and PwC show early adopters with stronger bottom-line results.

Watch from 09:14

Let AI stack-rank applicants. Beyond writing job descriptions and personalized outreach, platforms such as PeoplesHR now rank applicants by fit, saving recruiters hours on every role.

Watch from 13:17

Get the first six months right. A wrong hire or poor onboarding can cost 100% to 300% of salary. Use technology to make better hiring decisions and onboard people properly.

Watch from 14:18

Personalize learning by career stage. A new joiner, someone back from maternity leave and a newly promoted leader need different training. AI can recommend the right program for each person.

Watch from 26:32

AI can be a leader's private advisor. Tarvinder sees HR in many African organizations as transactional, with CEOs driving change alone. AI can take over routine tasks and give leaders a confidential sounding board.

Watch from 31:42

Ask the privacy questions first. AI output is only as good as the data you put in. Protect confidentiality, and follow how regulation develops as the first cases are tested.

Q&A highlights

Tarvinder recalled a UK cake factory where three generations of workers welcomed automation because it let the business export to Australia, Japan and Canada. People are complex, so HR will always need people. Luxsho added that repetitive roles are at risk, but technology mostly makes people more productive, as with bank tellers moved into new roles.
PeoplesHR has built AI into recruitment, onboarding and learning for organizations in Uganda, Kenya and Tanzania, including the Government of Uganda with half a million users. Self-service answers routine policy and leave questions, freeing HR for strategy. Tarvinder added that growing companies need systems, policies and clear interpretation of them.
Focus on one area. Find the biggest problem Tanzanian companies face, whether recruitment, pay, retention or incentives, and become really good at solving it. A product like that can succeed across the continent.
