WEBINAR SUMMARY · HR & PEOPLE

The whole world is your talent pool: how to find, attract and keep skills in emerging markets

FORMATOn-demand webinar
CONDUCTED ONTuesday, 6 June 2023
LANGUAGEEnglish

Charles H. Ferguson, General Manager APAC at Globalization Partners, explains why remote work has made the world one talent pool, and why small companies can now compete with Google for it. He covers building brand equity, the DICE model for remote hires, and the compliance work that has to come first.

WATCH THE RECORDING AND LEARN HOW TO

1. Build a brand that top candidates choose

2. Assess remote hires with the DICE model

3. Hire across borders without compliance risk

SPEAKERS
Charles H. Ferguson
General Manager – APAC, Globalization Partners
IS THIS SESSION FOR YOU?
FORHR leaders and founders hiring across borders or competing for scarce skills
LANGUAGEEnglish
TOPICSGlobal hiringEmployer brandRemote workComplianceTalent

Key takeaways

Watch from 03:07

Invest in brand equity. If the whole world is your talent pool, small and mid-sized companies can compete with Google or Microsoft. What sets you apart is how visible your brand, culture and values are.

Watch from 10:16

Revisit your mission, vision and values. Few companies have done this since the pandemic. Candidates want employers whose values match theirs, plus diversity and hybrid working, not just salary.

Watch from 14:20

Hire remote talent with DICE. Look for Determination to overcome obstacles, Insights drawn from too much information, Curiosity to keep learning, and Engagement with empathy across every level and culture.

Watch from 20:26

Think talent arbitrage, not labor arbitrage. Emerging markets are no longer just for cheaper labor. Oxford Economics found that in 2021, for the first time, the E7 had more university-educated people under 35 than the G7.

Watch from 22:31

Plan compliance before you hire. Understand local contracts, statutory benefits, probation, severance and data rules. Check salary data across three or four sources before you make an offer.

Watch from 34:46

HR's to-do list for a disrupted world. Reskilling, health and wellness, EQ training for leaders, a competency framework beyond the job, automation, updated rewards and remote-work policies aligned with IT.
