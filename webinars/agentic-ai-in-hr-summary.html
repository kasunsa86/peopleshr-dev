From answers to action: what agentic AI means for HR, and the questions to answer before you switch it on

FORMATOn-demand webinar
LENGTH68 min
CONDUCTED ONTue, 23 June 2026
LANGUAGEEnglish

Ioni Mendoza, CEO of Amorsolo Consulting, explains in plain terms how agentic AI differs from the AI tools HR already uses, where it helps across the employee lifecycle, and the governance questions to settle first. Manolo Garcia-Ramos then shows Lexi AI in PeoplesHR finding recruitment bottlenecks and overtime hotspots in seconds.

Key takeaways

Watch from 05:07

Doing nothing has a hidden cost. Early adopters are already building the learning curve and data advantage. As Ioni put it, AI won’t replace HR leaders, but HR leaders who use AI will replace those who don’t.

Watch from 08:11

Agentic AI takes a goal, not a prompt. Ask for "three shortlisted candidates interviewed by Monday" and it posts the job, screens and assesses applicants, and books interviews around the manager’s calendar, adapting if someone can’t make it.

Watch from 18:25

From reactive reports to early signals. Ioni's example: four stress-related leaves in one team, heavy overtime, 14 direct reports and no check-in for 60 days. Spotting the pattern lets HR step in before people leave.

Watch from 21:29

Three governance questions come first. Who is accountable for each decision, and which ones are high-risk? Where does a human stay in the loop? Is your data clean? With AI it is no longer garbage in, garbage out, but garbage in, landfill out.

Watch from 24:35

Avoid the common traps. Full autonomy too early, dirty data, settling for easy wins, no governance, unchecked bias, and no data processing agreement under the Data Privacy Act and National Privacy Commission rules.

Watch from 43:04

Lexi is built inside PeoplesHR. Lexi Insights answers leadership questions from your own data, while Smart Navigator and Super Agent help employees find things and get them done. Unlike ChatGPT, it knows your organization.
