WEBINAR SUMMARY · FINANCE & PAYROLL

Payroll automation starts before payroll: how to stay accurate, compliant and secure in the digital age

CONDUCTED ONFriday, 23 August 2024
LANGUAGEEnglish

Asitha Goonewardena, former Chief Product Owner, and Riyazi Samsudeen of PeoplesHR explain why digitalization is the route to cost-effective payroll, and why automation has to start with attendance, leave and rosters. They cover AI anomaly and fraud detection, compliance across 40 countries, data security, and where pay is heading.

1. Automate the inputs that feed payroll

Key takeaways

Watch from 02:04

Digitalization is the route to cost-effectiveness. An ADP survey ranked cost-effectiveness and digitalization equally as 2024's top payroll drivers. Asitha's view: map a digitalization roadmap with clear KPIs, and the savings follow.

Watch from 06:10

Accuracy is a given; experience is the difference. Employees want to see their earnings, overtime and deductions mid-month on their phone, and ask a chatbot about cut-offs and overtime rules instead of queuing at a payslip window.

Watch from 16:21

Automation starts with attendance and leave. Installing software is not automation. Let employees apply for leave and swap shifts themselves, so payroll gets clean inputs, including complex rules like Philippine holiday overtime and night differentials.

Watch from 21:27

Let AI watch for anomalies. Asitha cites research that payroll anomalies take 18 to 24 months to surface and cost around 5% of annual turnover. AI that learns normal pay patterns flags problems for the payroll officer.

Watch from 31:45

Compliance is a moving target. PeoplesHR works with legal and consulting partners to build each country’s rules into the system, updates them as laws change, and enforces limits such as overtime thresholds inside approval workflows.

Watch from 46:05

Controls stop fraud that hides for years. Ghost employees, inflated salaries and duplicate reimbursements are caught with thresholds, audit trails, statistical checks, receipt scanning, RPA and predictive analytics.
