WEBINAR SUMMARY · HR & PEOPLE

Technology can be bought, talent cannot: how HR leads workforce management in the age of AI

FORMATOn-demand webinar
CONDUCTED ONWednesday, 25 February 2026
LANGUAGEBahasa Indonesia

Dr. Rully C. Hamdani Nawawi, Director of PT Hutama Mandiri Cipta, explains why talent is the one advantage AI cannot copy, and why most HR transformations stall on people rather than software. The session then shows PeoplesHR's AI scoring and ranking job applicants in seconds. The session was held in Bahasa Indonesia.

WATCH THE RECORDING AND LEARN HOW TO

1. Move HR from administration to strategy

2. Clear the people barriers that stall HR transformation

3. Shortlist candidates faster with AI scoring

Key takeaways

Watch from 04:11

The talent shortage is already here. Dr. Rully cites Yamato Transport in Japan, which turned to AI routing after losing thousands of workers and cut delivery time by 20%. Companies are adopting AI because they have to.

Watch from 07:18

HR is now the strategic architect. HR is no longer the back office for payroll and attendance. Using data to understand people, for example to predict who may resign, lets HR shape company strategy.

Watch from 13:28

Transformation stalls on people, not software. Unclear strategy, tight budgets, cultures that resist change, legacy systems, leadership skill gaps and data fears hold organizations back. Good technology fails when the culture is not ready.

Watch from 15:32

What successful transformations share. They align with business strategy, invest in people rather than software, have unwavering leadership commitment, and focus on user adoption.

Watch from 18:36

People using AI will replace those who do not. AI will not replace humans, but analysts who use AI tools will replace colleagues who refuse to learn them. Adaptive organizations pull ahead.

Watch from 22:38

Let AI shortlist the applicants. In PeoplesHR you set weighted parameters for each role. AI parses each CV, scores every applicant and explains the score, so a hundred applications can be sorted in seconds.

Q&A highlights

At LG Electronics, Dr. Rully saw AI used in performance management, mainly for goal setting. Recruitment used AI only at the database level, not to assess incoming talent.
In PeoplesHR, AI aggregates performance and competency results, predicts performance and potential scores, and maps employees onto the 9-box grid automatically. It also flags who is ready now or ready soon for succession, and matches skills to roles.
Yes. AI can bring together attendance, sales and other data and rank people, but the final call, especially on promotions, stays with people. AI makes the work easier; it does not take over the decision.
