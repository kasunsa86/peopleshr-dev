WEBINAR SUMMARY · FINANCE & PAYROLL

Payday is a trust issue: what Philippine HR needs to fix in payroll before 2026

FORMATOn-demand webinar
CONDUCTED ONThursday, 18 September 2025
LANGUAGEEnglish

Gigs Patano, CHRO of HIJO Resources Corporation, lays out what manual payroll really costs: errors, HR burnout and lost trust when pay is a day late. Riyazi Samsudeen then shows how automation handles special holidays, night shifts and payslip questions, with payroll that runs in hours.

WATCH THE RECORDING AND LEARN HOW TO

1. Cut the manual steps that cause payroll errors

2. Stay compliant with SSS, PhilHealth, Pag-IBIG and BIR

3. Answer payslip questions without a trip to HR

Key takeaways

Watch from 03:14

Manual steps are where errors start. Payroll errors affect one in four employees worldwide, and Excel-based payroll can take up to 80% longer than automated systems. Data split across biometrics, leave tools and spreadsheets makes it worse.

Watch from 07:11

Payroll is a trust issue. In Gigs' own engagement survey, staff had no complaints as long as pay arrived on the 15th and 30th. A single day's delay costs far more goodwill than it looks.

Watch from 13:41

99% accuracy is not good enough. Future-ready payroll needs 100% accuracy every cut-off, books ready for a DOLE inspection at any time, one shared truth for employees and managers, and a system that scales up or down.

Watch from 21:43

Treat every cycle as audit day. Unify attendance, overtime and leave without re-entry, so there are no special runs and no year-end tax refunds or extra deductions, even for mid-year joiners.

Watch from 46:10

Let the system handle the edge cases. Mark a special holiday on the calendar and the computation changes for every affected shift, including graveyard shifts that cross midnight and regional daily rates.

Watch from 48:29

Payslip questions answer themselves. Ask Lexi why a transport allowance came in at ₱1,000 instead of ₱2,000 and it shows the days worked and the rate, with no HR counter. It also understands Tagalog and local dialects.
