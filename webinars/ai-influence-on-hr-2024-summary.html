WEBINAR SUMMARY · HR & PEOPLE

AI will take the tasks, not the HR team: practical ways to use it across the employee lifecycle

FORMATOn-demand webinar
CONDUCTED ONWednesday, 24 July 2024
LANGUAGEEnglish

Luxsho Logan, former VP of Sales at PeoplesHR, traces AI from machine learning to generative tools, and explains why repetitive HR work will change while strategic work stays human. He shares practical uses across recruitment, onboarding, learning and employee experience, and how to measure the return.

WATCH THE RECORDING AND LEARN HOW TO

1. Use AI in recruitment, onboarding and learning today

2. Measure the impact of every AI investment

3. Know where AI falls short and humans stay in charge

Key takeaways

Watch from 06:11

Waiting is the bigger risk. In Gartner research, 76% of HR leaders said they would fall behind competitors within 12 to 24 months without AI, and more than half of CEOs feared their company might not exist in ten years.

Watch from 07:12

AI takes tasks, not careers. Repetitive admin work will be automated, but strategic and creative work stays human. Bank tellers were not let go when online banking arrived: their jobs moved to more complex work.

Watch from 10:16

Start with recruitment. Use generative AI to write bias-free job descriptions and personalized candidate messages. Newer tools target postings at the right audience and look beyond the resume when shortlisting.

Watch from 17:25

Automate the first six months. New hires are most likely to leave early. Automating schedules, introductions and learning plans from contract signing helps avoid a bad hire costing 100% to 300% of salary.

Watch from 23:34

Engagement should not take three months. Annual or quarterly surveys can take three months or more to turn into insight. AI can flag disengagement from everyday signals in real time, so HR can act sooner.

Watch from 26:37

Measure everything you automate. If you can't measure an AI project, you won't see its return. Invest in data literacy first, and focus on speed and efficiency.
