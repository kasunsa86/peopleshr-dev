WEBINAR SUMMARY · PRODUCT

Empower, don't centralize: how mobile HR reaches the frontline teams who never sit at a desk

FORMATOn-demand webinar
CONDUCTED ONFriday, 10 April 2026
LANGUAGEEnglish

Unlock This Recording

Fill in your details to watch the full session. Takeaways, chapters and Q&A on this page stay open.

Riyazi Samsudeen follows one working day across a Laguna factory, a Cebu hotel, a Manila delivery route and a BGC BPO floor. Each stop shows what changes when HR moves onto employees' phones: attendance and leave validated the same day, payslips and claims in their pocket, and payroll that runs without month-end chaos.

WATCH THE RECORDING AND LEARN HOW TO

1. Push HR out to employees

2. Clock in where the work is

3. Put pay and performance in every pocket

Key takeaways

Watch from 01:49

Push HR out to employees. When staff mark their own attendance and leave and it's verified the same day, payroll starts from clean data instead of month-end chaos.

Watch from 05:38

Count the cost of unreachable HR. Buddy punching, last-minute absences, paper appraisals, payslip disputes and grievances that quietly pile up until they blow up.

Watch from 12:18

Clock in where the work is. GPS plus a selfie face check on the phone, or keep existing biometric devices and feed their raw data straight in.

Watch from 14:02

Nudge, don't block. Before applying for leave, staff see that 40% of their team is already off. The app also flags people who haven't taken leave in months.

Watch from 15:47

Put pay and performance in every pocket. Drivers check payslips and claim meal allowances from a photo. Frontline staff complete appraisals on mobile, with AI drafting their goals.
