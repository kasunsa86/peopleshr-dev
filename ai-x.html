AI-Powered HR with Lexi Assistant

✦ The Everyday Hero

Designed for Humans, Built for Impact

You didn't become an HR leader to click through endless screens or chase people for overdue approvals. You're here to drive performance, develop talent, and create workplaces where people thrive.

PeoplesHR X puts the focus back where it belongs, on your people, not your platform.

Search Like You Think

Forget hunting through menus. Type "pending performance reviews" or "Sarah's leave balance" & get instant answers. No training needed, no user manual required. It's like having your go to generative AI for your HR data.

Talk Instead of Type

Running between meetings? Say "Apply for annual leave, December 23rd to January 2nd" and watch it happen. Voice commands for the tasks that eat up your time. Because sometimes speaking is faster than clicking.

Evaluations on the go

Complete performance reviews on the go. Approve requests from the coffee queue. Everything you need, wherever you. No more "I'll do this when I get back to my desk."

AI that Speaks HR

Need to draft development goals? The AI suggests relevant options based on role and performance history. Writing performance feedback? Get intelligent prompts that help you say what you mean, professionally. It's like having an experienced HR mentor in your corner.

✦ Meet Lexi – Your HR Co-Pilot

Smarter Workflows, Instant Results

Think of Lexi as that experienced HR colleague who always knows the answer. Except she's available 24/7, never gets tired of questions, and remembers every policy detail perfectly.

"How much annual leave do I get?" "What's our flexible working policy?" "How do I book training?" Lexi handles these instantly, with answers specific to your company and location. Your employees get help right away, you get your time back.

Rank CVs for your latest hire. Draft job descriptions. Create recruitment checklists. Schedule interview panels. All the important groundwork that frees up your time for high-impact strategic decisions.

New manager doesn't know how to conduct a performance review? Lexi walks them through it step-by-step. One search, instant answer. Support when you need it, invisible when you don't.

Multi-country policies? Different employment types? Various leave entitlements? Lexi knows your exact setup and gives the right answer to the right person, every time. No more "let me check and get back to you."

✦ The Power of People

Hire Smarter, Grow Stronger – Where Performance Meets Potential

The Reality Check: You post a job, get 200 CVs, spend hours screening, interview 10 people, and still wonder if you picked the right one. Then you cross your fingers and hope they stick around.

The Solution: Let AI do the heavy lifting so you can focus on the human decisions that actually matter.

Elevate Your Hiring Game

Intelligent Job Creation AI analyzes your top performers to write job descriptions that attract the right people. Insights from your actual success stories, not generic templates.

Smart CV Screening Rank candidates on likelihood of success in your environment. See the genuinely qualified top 10, not a random sample from 200 applications.

Video Interview Intelligence Beyond what they say—how they communicate, their engagement style, their presence. Objective insights that support your instincts and eliminate bias.

Performance Prediction Connect recruitment data to actual performance outcomes. See which hiring indicators predicted your best hires, then use that intelligence.

Predictive Insights That Actually Help

Retention Intelligence "Sarah shows engagement patterns similar to your highest performers." Have the development conversation now, not after she's gone.

Succession Planning That Works AI identifies your future stars before you desperately need them. Performance trends and leadership indicators reveal who's ready.

Pattern Recognition Spot trends in workload, engagement, and team dynamics before small issues become big problems. Act proactively, not reactively.

Performance Management That Flows

Mobile Performance Excellence Complete meaningful reviews anywhere with AI assistance. Performance management that fits real schedules, not ideal ones.

Integrated Talent Development Connect performance insights directly to development planning. Every conversation leads to growth.

Always-On Support 24/7 support handles routine questions and requests. Your people get help when they need it, not just when you're available.
