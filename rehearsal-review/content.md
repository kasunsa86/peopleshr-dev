## Key takeaways
1. **Count the 13th month inside the ₱90,000 ceiling.** It already includes 13th-month pay and other benefits, so bonuses above it are taxable even if the offer says tax-exempt. (17:40)
2. **Use basic salary only.** Total basic salary earned January to December, less absences, divided by 12. Allowances, overtime and premium pay stay out. (20:26)
3. **Start in September or October.** Validate master data first, reconcile and test-run annualization in November, and keep December for final checks, reporting and payout. (27:41)
4. **Annualize every month.** Computing tax monthly as if for the full year avoids underpayment and heavy December deductions, so take-home pay stays steady. (30:03)
5. **Build a preparer, reviewer and approver chain.** Be ready to show where a number came from, what changed, who approved it and how to reproduce it. (32:29)
6. **Catch problems before payroll.** Late overtime approvals, unreported promotions and leave filed after cutoff all start upstream; payroll is only where they land. (42:42)

## Q&A highlights (payroll and tax answers flagged for a marketing check [FLAG 3])
1. **Do lates and undertime affect 13th-month pay?** (47:37) Yes. It is based on basic salary actually earned in the year, so unpaid absences, lates and undertime that reduce basic salary also reduce the 13th-month amount.
2. **Is the BIR schedule fixed?** (48:12) It is the annual schedule unless the BIR issues an advisory or extension, which in the speakers' experience often comes two or three days before a deadline.
3. **What counts as an anomaly?** (50:50) Something that looks unusual against an employee's usual pattern and past payroll data. It isn't necessarily wrong; it is worth checking before you run payroll.
4. **Which year-end work is worth outsourcing?** (52:48) Sharon recommended annualization, the most time-consuming task for large teams. An outside partner also acts as a check on the year's work and can advise on monthly payroll.
5. **What security standards should an HRIS vendor have?** (54:31) Ask for ISO 27001, a SOC 1 or SOC 2 report, and Philippine data privacy certification.
6. **How do you handle a flood of payroll questions?** (57:10) Set an SLA, such as three days from payslip release, and print it on the payslip. An HRIS chatbot can answer routine questions any time.
