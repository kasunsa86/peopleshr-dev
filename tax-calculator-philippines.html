How withholding tax is calculated in the Philippines

Each payday the employer deducts the employee's SSS, PhilHealth and Pag-IBIG contributions from gross compensation. What's left, less any non-taxable benefits, is taxable compensation, and the BIR withholding tax table is applied to it. At year end the tax is recomputed on the full year using the annual table.

13th month pay, bonuses and year-end annualization

13th month pay and other benefits are tax-free up to ₱90,000 a year combined. On a ₱50,000 salary, the ₱50,000 13th month pay is fully exempt and annual tax stays at ₱54,820. Add a ₱60,000 performance bonus and benefits total ₱110,000: the ₱20,000 above the cap is taxed at 20%, raising annual tax to ₱58,820. That ₱4,000 is collected in the year-end annualization, along with any other difference between tax withheld and tax due.

Philippines tax calculator FAQs

How much tax is deducted from a ₱30,000 salary in the Philippines?

On a ₱30,000 monthly basic salary the employee pays ₱1,500 SSS, ₱750 PhilHealth and ₱200 Pag-IBIG, leaving ₱27,550 taxable compensation. Withholding tax is 15% of the ₱6,717 above ₱20,833, or ₱1,007.55, so take-home pay is ₱26,542.45.

Who is exempt from withholding tax?

Employees whose taxable compensation is ₱250,000 a year or less (about ₱20,833 a month) pay no income tax. Minimum wage earners are also exempt on their statutory minimum wage, holiday pay, overtime, night shift differential and hazard pay.

Is 13th month pay taxable in the Philippines?

13th month pay and other benefits, such as bonuses and productivity incentives, are tax-exempt up to ₱90,000 a year combined. Only the amount above ₱90,000 is added to taxable compensation, usually in the year-end annualization.

How is a bonus taxed?

Add the bonus to your 13th month pay and other benefits for the year. Whatever exceeds ₱90,000 is taxed at your annual marginal rate. Enter the total in the “13th Month & Bonuses” field of the year-end annualization panel to see the effect.

What is year-end annualization?

Monthly withholding is an estimate. In the last payroll of the year the employer recomputes the employee’s tax on the full year’s taxable compensation using the annual BIR table, then deducts any shortfall or refunds any excess. The result is reported on BIR Form 2316.

What is BIR Form 2316?

BIR Form 2316 is the Certificate of Compensation Payment / Tax Withheld that employers give employees each year. It shows gross compensation, non-taxable and taxable amounts, tax due and tax withheld. This calculator fills an illustrative version you can print; it is not for official filing.

Does the calculator use 2026 tax rates?

Yes. The TRAIN law withholding table that took effect on 1 January 2023 still applies in 2026. The calculator also uses the 2025 SSS schedule (15% with a ₱35,000 maximum Monthly Salary Credit), PhilHealth at 5% and Pag-IBIG with a ₱10,000 maximum fund salary.

Is my salary data stored?

No. All calculations run in your browser. Nothing you enter, including bulk CSV uploads, is sent to or stored by PeoplesHR.

Philippines payroll reference

Rates & Rules

SSS, effective January 2025

  • 15% total contribution: 5% employee and 10% employer SS/MPF share.
  • Monthly Salary Credit from ₱5,000 to ₱35,000 in ₱500 steps.
  • MSC above ₱20,000 is allocated to MPF.
  • Employer-only EC is ₱10 below ₱15,000 MSC and ₱30 from ₱15,000 MSC upward.

PhilHealth

  • 5% total premium using Basic Salary + Taxable Allowances.
  • ₱10,000 floor and ₱100,000 ceiling.
  • 2.5% employee and 2.5% employer shares.

Pag-IBIG / HDMF, effective February 2024

  • Employee 1% at ₱1,500 or below; otherwise 2%.
  • Employer 2%.
  • Maximum fund salary ₱10,000; maximum employee share ₱200.

BIR withholding, January 2023 onwards

  • Monthly taxable compensation up to ₱20,833: zero.
  • Marginal rates then progress through 15%, 20%, 25%, 30% and 35%.
  • Combined exemption cap for 13th-month pay and other benefits: ₱90,000.

Recent changes reflected

  • SSS 2025 rate and ₱35,000 maximum MSC.
  • Pag-IBIG ₱10,000 maximum fund salary.
  • Revised BIR withholding brackets effective from January 2023.
