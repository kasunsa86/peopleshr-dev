Rates & Rules

SSS, effective January 2025

  • 15% total contribution: 5% employee and 10% employer SS/MPF share.
  • Monthly Salary Credit from ₱5,000 to ₱35,000 in ₱500 steps.
  • MSC above ₱20,000 is allocated to MPF.
  • Employer-only EC is ₱10 below ₱15,000 MSC and ₱30 from ₱15,000 MSC upward.

PhilHealth

  • 5% total premium using Basic Salary + Taxable Allowances.
  • ₱10,000 floor and ₱100,000 ceiling.
  • 2.5% employee and 2.5% employer shares.

Pag-IBIG / HDMF, effective February 2024

  • Employee 1% at ₱1,500 or below; otherwise 2%.
  • Employer 2%.
  • Maximum fund salary ₱10,000; maximum employee share ₱200.

BIR withholding, January 2023 onwards

  • Monthly taxable compensation up to ₱20,833: zero.
  • Marginal rates then progress through 15%, 20%, 25%, 30% and 35%.
  • Combined exemption cap for 13th-month pay and other benefits: ₱90,000.

Recent changes reflected

  • SSS 2025 rate and ₱35,000 maximum MSC.
  • Pag-IBIG ₱10,000 maximum fund salary.
  • Revised BIR withholding brackets effective from January 2023.
