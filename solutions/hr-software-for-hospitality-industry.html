Why Hospitality HR Needs a Different Approach

Hospitality is fast-paced, guest-facing, and highly dependent on people. When HR breaks down, service slows, compliance risks increase, and guest trust erodes. Managing a multi-shift, multilingual, deskless workforce requires more than a generic HR system.

The Hospitality Reality.

🤝

Labour costs = 65–70% of hotel operating expenses (PwC).

📊

Frontline attrition averages 45% in ASEAN hospitality (ILO).

💰

Payroll is complex: overtime, split shifts, tips, service charges, union rules.

📱

80% of employees are deskless and need mobile-first HR.

📋

Seasonal peaks create scheduling and payroll strain.

Real impact  —  "Employee data retrieval that used to take 5 minutes now happens in under 1 minute — that's 80% productivity improvement."
