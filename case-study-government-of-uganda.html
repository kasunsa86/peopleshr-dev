CASE STUDY

A National Government digitizes recruitment-to-retirement HR across 235+ institutions for 420,000+ public officers and retirees

How the Government of Uganda replaced fragmented, decentralized HR records with one government-wide PeoplesHR powered Human Capital Management platform, unifying the full employee lifecycle from recruitment to retirement while strengthening workforce planning, National ID authentication and real-time HR decision-making across 235+ institutions.

COMPANY Government of Uganda, Ministry of Public Service
INDUSTRY Government / Public Service
COMPANY SIZE 350,000+ active + 70,000+ retired employees
LOCATION Uganda

Challenge

The Government of Uganda operates through more than 235 institutions, spanning ministries, departments, agencies and local governments. With 350,000+ employees in active service and 70,000+ retired government officials, the government needed an interoperable, scalable Human Capital Management System to support accurate HR information, strategic decision-making and its wider Vision 2040 digital transformation agenda.

  • No centralized employee record: Personnel data was fragmented across institutions, slowing decisions and making it difficult to access officer records when cross-institution reference was required.
  • Wage-bill integrity risk: Public officers and retirees needed to be authenticated against the National ID database to support accurate wage budgeting and reduce payments to non-existent public officers, also referred to as ghost employees.
  • Slow pension processing: Semi-automated payroll processes took many months to pay pensions to retired employees or their beneficiaries.
  • Slow recruitment and workforce planning: Recruitment cycles could take up to 12 months, while identifying resource requirements across institutions could take more than six months.
  • Limited visibility and control: Uganda's decentralized structure required better oversight, monitoring and real-time access to accurate HR information across institutions.

PeoplesHR Solution

Delivered through a joint venture of Sybyl Uganda, Sybyl Kenya and PeoplesHR, the Government of Uganda implemented an integrated Human Capital Management platform to support the full public-service employee lifecycle. The system standardized HR and finance processes, improved data reliability, enabled integration with third-party systems and gave authorized users access to accurate, real-time HR information.

  • Single source of truth: PeoplesHR enabled a single master staff record containing biodata and service information for each public officer, accessible to authorized users.
  • Integrated and authenticated: Automated authentication of public officers and retirees against the National ID database helped reduce ghost-worker risk and improve payroll and pension accuracy.
  • Full lifecycle coverage: The platform supported recruitment, onboarding, employee information, mobile self-service, leave, performance, training, succession, payroll, pension, offboarding and analytics.
  • Third-party integration and auditability: Integration with third-party systems reduced duplication, manual intervention and error risk, while providing system-generated audit trails for accountability and transparency.
  • Faster planning and policy alignment: HR planning consolidation enabled institutions to identify new recruits, retirees and training needs far faster, while dashboards and reporting supported data-driven monitoring and policy alignment.

Results

The rollout marks the beginning of a government-wide HR transformation — one of the largest public-sector HCM deployments PeoplesHR has supported to date.

"This has marked the initiation of our HR transformation journey within the whole Government of Uganda, implemented by the joint venture of Sybyl Uganda, Sybyl Kenya and PeoplesHR as our HR solutions provider."

AM Mr. Allan Muhereza — Commissioner HRMS, Ministry of Public Service
