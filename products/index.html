Integrated Platform Architecture

Seamless Data Flow Across All Modules

Single Hire, Complete Integration

When you hire an employee, their information automatically flows to payroll, time tracking, performance management, and mobile access. No duplicate data entry, no manual synchronisation.

One-Time Updates

Employee promotion? Role change? Location transfer? Update once and watch the change cascade through access permissions, salary records, org charts, and team notifications.

Automated Workflows

Attendance records feed payroll calculations. Leave requests update shift schedules. Performance reviews inform development plans. Time logs allocate to cost centres. Every module communicates seamlessly.

Implementation & Support

Everything you need to get up and running — and stay that way.

Integration Capabilities

Connect PeoplesHR with your existing technology ecosystem through pre-built connectors for common ERP and accounting systems, or leverage the modern API architecture for custom integrations. Access extended functionality through a third-party app marketplace, and streamline document workflows with integrated digital signature capabilities.

Customisation Options

Adapt PeoplesHR to your unique business requirements with configurable workflows that require no coding expertise. Create custom fields and data structures through Dynamic Data Structure (DDS) capabilities, design adaptable approval hierarchies, and leverage industry-specific configuration templates.

Security & Compliance

Protect sensitive employee data with enterprise-grade security featuring role-based access controls and comprehensive audit trails. All data is encrypted and stored securely, with full compliance to international data protection standards. Region-specific labour law configurations ensure your operations meet local regulatory requirements.

Ongoing Support

Receive continuous assistance through dedicated customer success management and responsive technical support for troubleshooting. Access comprehensive training resources and documentation to maximise platform utilisation, while benefiting from regular platform updates and enhancements that keep your system current.
