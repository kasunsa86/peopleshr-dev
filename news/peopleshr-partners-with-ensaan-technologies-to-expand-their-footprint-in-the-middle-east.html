PeoplesHR Partners with Ensaan Technologies to Expand their Footprint in the Middle East

September 23, 2024 · 3 min read

PeoplesHR, a global leader in HR solutions, is pleased to announce its strategic partnership with Ensaan Technologies, a leading provider of HR systems and talent management solutions in the Middle East. This collaboration marks a significant step forward in expanding PeoplesHR’s presence in the region, offering clients a comprehensive and cutting-edge HR solution tailored to meet the evolving needs of businesses.

The partnership between PeoplesHR and Ensaan Technologies is poised to deliver unparalleled value to organizations across the Middle East, combining PeoplesHR’s robust HR solution with Ensaan’s deep expertise in deploying HR systems from leading global vendors. Together, the two companies will provide clients with a holistic HR solution that integrates advanced technology with industry-leading practices, ensuring that businesses can optimize their human capital management processes and achieve their strategic objectives.

Sampath Jayasundara, CEO of PeoplesHR, expressed his enthusiasm for the partnership, stating, “We are excited to partner with Ensaan Technologies, a company that shares our vision of transforming HR operations through innovative technology. This collaboration will allow us to extend our footprint in the Middle Eastern market, providing businesses with the tools they need to enhance their HR capabilities and drive growth. By joining forces with Ensaan, we are confident that we can deliver exceptional value to our clients, helping them navigate the complexities of HR management in an increasingly dynamic and competitive environment.”

“We are thrilled to partner with PeoplesHR to offer cutting-edge HR solutions that align with the region’s rapidly evolving business landscape,” said Vinod, Philip, CEO of Ensaan Technologies. “Together, we will help companies in the Middle East to accelerate their HR transformation, empowering them to focus on what matters most—their people.”

This partnership underscores PeoplesHR’s commitment to expanding its global reach and delivering localized solutions that cater to the unique needs of businesses in different regions. With Ensaan Technologies’ deep understanding of the Middle Eastern market and its strong relationships with leading HR technology vendors, PeoplesHR is well-positioned to become a key player in the region’s HR technology landscape.

The collaboration will focus on delivering a seamless and integrated HR experience for clients, offering a range of solutions that cover everything from core HR functions to advanced analytics, talent management, and employee engagement. By leveraging the strengths of both PeoplesHR and Ensaan Technologies, businesses in the Middle East can look forward to a new era of HR excellence, characterized by enhanced efficiency, agility, and strategic alignment.

About PeoplesHR

PeoplesHR is a leading end-to-end HR solution, and the first enterprise software product company listed on the Colombo Stock Exchange. For over 26 years, Transforming HR over the last 26 years, PeoplesHR has supported over 1600 HR departments in over 40 countries across 20 industries while operating across 7 nations and one of the most trusted names in the HR Tech industry in Asia Pacific, Africa, and Middle Eastern regions, capturing all HR needs with HR solutions along with an HR ecosystem.

About Ensaan Technologies

Ensaan Technologies is a leading provider of HR software & Talent Management solutions, technologies and services across the Middle East and African region. Ensaan represents carefully chosen Global leaders in HR Management systems and talent software solutions in the region. With each passing month, the landscape of solutions available for HR services evolves and changes. New technologies enter the market, competitive strategies shift and vendors themselves may be in a constant state of flux. Acquisitions can lead to duplication and fragmentation, even as legacy technologies lose support. All the while, executives demand more and more functionality and service.

Ensaan’s structured approach begins by analyzing what HR technology is already in place – as well as validating the business and technology requirements. From this study, we help our clients chart a path forward to synchronize HR priorities with available supporting technologies – and assist in alignment with the overall business strategy.

For more information about the partnership and the solutions offered, please visit www.peopleshr.com and www.ensaantech.com
